CEIR — Govt Lost Phone Portal
Phone chori/kho jaye to Government of India ke official CEIR portal se block/track karayein
1
Nazdiki police station me FIR/complaint darj karayein
2
ceir.gov.in pe jayein aur "Block Stolen/Lost Mobile" section kholein
3
Mobile number, IMEI, FIR details aur device info bharein
4
Request submit karein — phone block ho jayega, kisi bhi network par kaam nahi karega
5
Phone mil jaye to "Unblock" request dalein
📎 Official Website: ceir.gov.in
📞 Helpline: 14422
Source: Department of Telecommunications, Government of India
