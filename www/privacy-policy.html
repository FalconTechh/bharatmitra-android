Privacy Policy — BharatMitra AI

Last Updated: August 2026

BharatMitra AI ("hum", "humara app") aapki privacy ka poora khayal rakhta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsi jaankari collect karte hain, kaise use karte hain, aur aapke rights kya hain.

⚠️ BharatMitra AI ek independent app hai. Ye Government of India, kisi bhi private hospital, ya kisi e-commerce platform se affiliated ya authorized nahi hai.

1. Hum kya jaankari collect karte hain

  • Account Info: Naam, email, profile photo (agar aap upload karein)
  • Camera/Photos: Scam Guardian ke QR/screenshot checks ya profile photo ke liye — sirf jab aap khud camera/gallery se koi photo choose karte hain
  • Business Manager: Sales, stock, customer, invoice data jo aap khud enter karte hain
  • Family Safety Hub: Family member ke naam/phone (jo aap khud add karein), aapki live location (SIRF jab aap khud toggle ON karein)
  • Hospital Helper: Hospital search/use information aur aapke khud enter kiye gaye details
  • JanSeva: Service information jo aap khud enter karte hain
  • My Queue: Token history, business registration details (agar aap business owner hain)
  • Scam Guardian: Aapke check kiye gaye links/messages/QR inputs ka content analysis ke liye process hota hai; search/check input ko permanently store karna app ka purpose nahi hai
  • Device/Push Token: Notification bhejne ke liye ek anonymous device identifier (OneSignal ke through)

Hum kabhi bhi aapke Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, ya Microphone access nahi karte.

2. Location Data

Location sirf tab collect hoti hai jab:

  • Aap Family Safety Hub me "Live Location Sharing" khud ON karein — is case me native Android app me location background me bhi update hoti rehti hai (jab tak toggle ON hai), taaki family aapki live location dekh sake, chahe app screen band ho
  • Aap Hospital Helper ya My Queue me "Nearby Dhoondein" button dabate hain
  • Aap SOS button use karte hain

Background location sirf Family Safety Hub ke "Live Location Sharing" toggle ke through, aapki explicit permission ke baad hi chalti hai — aap ise kisi bhi waqt band kar sakte hain, jiske baad location update hona turant ruk jaata hai. Iske alawa app kabhi bhi location track nahi karta.

3A. BharatMitra Search

Search feature me aapki query internet par available third-party/open search services ko bheji ja sakti hai taaki relevant results mil sakein. Search results third-party websites se aate hain aur un websites ki apni privacy policies hoti hain. BharatMitra un external websites ke data practices ko control nahi karta.

Recent search history app ke device-local storage me rakhi ja sakti hai taaki aap recent searches dobara use kar saken. Aap app data clear karke ise hata sakte hain.

BharatMitra Search government, financial, medical ya emergency information ke liye official source ko verify karne ka reminder deta hai; search result ko official endorsement na samjhein.

3. Notifications

Login karne par app aapke device ko push notification bhejne ke liye register karta hai (jaise queue token updates), taaki app band hone par bhi zaroori alerts mil sakein. Aap ise Settings → Notifications se band kar sakte hain, ya Android Settings se app-level notification permission hata sakte hain.

4. Data Sharing

Hum aapka data kisi ko bechte nahi hain. Data process karne ke liye hum sirf in trusted service providers ka use karte hain:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, aur push notification delivery ke liye
  • OneSignal — push notification delivery ke liye (sirf anonymous device token share hota hai)

5. Ads

BharatMitra AI me koi third-party advertisement nahi dikhaya jaata.

6. Data Kaise Store Hoti Hai

Sara data Google Firebase (Firestore) par store hota hai, jo industry-standard security use karta hai. Har user ka data sirf usi user ko dikhta hai — hum strict access-control rules follow karte hain.

7. Sensitive Data Handling

  • Hum kabhi bhi government ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport number) collect nahi karte
  • Medical reports sirf aapko dikhte hain, kisi aur ko nahi
  • Family member ki location sirf tab dikhti hai jab wo khud sharing ON karein

8. Bachon Ki Privacy (Children's Privacy)

BharatMitra AI 18 saal se kam umar ke bachon ke liye design nahi kiya gaya hai aur hum jaan-boojh kar bachon se data collect nahi karte.

9. Third-Party Services

Hum in services ka use karte hain:

  • Google Firebase — authentication aur database
  • Google Safe Browsing API — link safety check (Scam Guardian)
  • OpenStreetMap — local place/service discovery
  • DuckDuckGo Instant Answer API and Wikipedia public API — BharatMitra Search discovery results

10. Aapke Rights

  • Kisi bhi time apna data delete kar sakte hain (respective tab me Delete option se)
  • Account delete karne ke liye Help & Support se contact karein
  • Live Location sharing kisi bhi time OFF kar sakte hain

11. Contact

Kisi bhi sawal ke liye app ke andar Help & Support section use karein.
