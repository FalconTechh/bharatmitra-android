Privacy Policy — BharatMitra AI

Last Updated: August 2026

BharatMitra AI ("hum", "humara app") aapki privacy ka poora khayal rakhta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum kaunsi jaankari collect karte hain, kaise use karte hain, aur aapke rights kya hain.

⚠️ BharatMitra AI ek independent app hai. Ye Government of India, kisi bhi private hospital, ya kisi e-commerce platform se affiliated ya authorized nahi hai.

1. Hum kya jaankari collect karte hain

  • Account Info: Naam, email, profile photo (agar aap upload karein)
  • Business Manager: Sales, stock, customer, invoice data jo aap khud enter karte hain
  • Family Safety Hub: Family member ke naam/phone (jo aap khud add karein), aapki live location (SIRF jab aap khud toggle ON karein)
  • Hospital Helper: Aapke upload kiye gaye medical reports/photos, hospital naam aur date
  • JanSeva: Document type aur expiry date (ID number kabhi collect nahi karte)
  • My Queue: Token history, business registration details (agar aap business owner hain)
  • Scam Guardian: Aapke check kiye gaye links/messages ka text (analysis ke liye, permanently store nahi hota)

2. Location Data

Location sirf tab collect hoti hai jab:

  • Aap Family Safety Hub me "Live Location Sharing" khud ON karein — is case me location Firestore me update hoti hai jab tak app khula hai aur toggle ON hai
  • Aap Hospital Helper ya My Queue me "Nearby Dhoondein" button dabate hain
  • Aap SOS button use karte hain

Hum background me kabhi bhi location track nahi karte — website hone ki wajah se ye technically possible bhi nahi hai.

3. Data Kaise Store Hoti Hai

Sara data Google Firebase (Firestore) par store hota hai, jo industry-standard security use karta hai. Har user ka data sirf usी user ko dikhta hai — hum strict access-control rules follow karte hain.

4. Sensitive Data Handling

  • Hum kabhi bhi government ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport number) collect nahi karte
  • Medical reports sirf aapko dikhte hain, kisi aur ko nahi
  • Family member ki location sirf tab dikhti hai jab wo khud sharing ON karein

5. Third-Party Services

Hum in services ka use karte hain:

  • Google Firebase — authentication aur database
  • Google Safe Browsing API — link safety check (Scam Guardian)
  • OpenStreetMap — hospital/business location data

6. Aapke Rights

  • Kisi bhi time apna data delete kar sakte hain (respective tab me Delete option se)
  • Account delete karne ke liye Help & Support se contact karein
  • Live Location sharing kisi bhi time OFF kar sakte hain

7. Contact

Kisi bhi sawal ke liye app ke andar Help & Support section use karein.
